Short games, long season.

Built around a student week: two match nights that end before 10 PM, and a table that stays alive until the last round.

8

Department teams

One squad per department, 14 players each, all current students of Yashodha University.

10overs

A side

Power-play in the first three overs. Each bowler can bowl a maximum of two.

2

Nights a week

Wednesday and Sunday, first ball at 7:00 PM. Two matches back to back on each night.

8weeks

To the final

Seven rounds of league play, then semi-finals on the Wednesday and the final on the Sunday.

Points

Win 2, tie or washout 1, loss 0. Net run rate separates teams on equal points.

Ball

White leather ball, coloured kit, sightscreens lit. Umpires from the university panel.

Weather

A washed-out night moves to the next free Saturday. The schedule below updates when that happens.
